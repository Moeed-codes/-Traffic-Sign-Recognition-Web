      About

      Leaf Ray Net Detection uses a trained convolutional neural network to identify plant leaf diseases. Upload a leaf photo and get a ranked set of predictions, heatmap (Grad-CAM), and actionable treatment tips.

      How it works
      1. Upload a clear photo of the leaf.
      2. The model analyzes patterns and returns top predictions with confidence.
      3. View Grad-CAM heatmap to see regions influencing the prediction.
      Supported plants & examples

      Includes many crops like apple, grape, pepper, strawberry and 30+ disease classes (based on the New Plant Diseases dataset).
